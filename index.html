I am a PhD researcher at the University of Helsinki and the Finnish Centre of Excellence in Tax Systems Research (FIT). I study how climate change and local pollution affect people and the environment, and how policies can be designed to better address these challenges. My research draws on both contemporary and historical evidence, spanning topics related to energy transitions: the legacies of mining, the health effects of environmental change, and the uptake of clean technologies. In spring 2026, I visited the Paris School of Economics, hosted by Hélène Ollivier.
